## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Watch capture uses the canonical circular add treatment
The Watch capture surface SHALL present one stable circular plus control with a slightly darkened semantic green fill, a thin lighter-green border, and a white plus symbol.

#### Scenario: Render the Watch add control
- **WHEN** the Watch capture surface is idle or displays transient status text
- **THEN** the centered add control retains its size and position with the darker fill and lighter outline treatment
